How it works

A BATTLE

Take the enemy HQ and hold your own. An HQ falls when enemy troops hold its ground for 40 seconds; a bar at the top shows the count. Driven off, it runs back down. Each phase lasts 60 seconds: before it you build, buy and give orders, then it plays out on its own. At the end, watch the replay.

GROUND

The battle is the circle (1, 2 or 3 km across); the grey ring around it is scenery. You can only build on ground you hold, tinted your colour. Troops that clearly hold ground take it. Supply each phase comes from the ground you hold.

DEPLOYING

New units arrive in your start area. Engineers can build a FOB near where they stand; it is ready the next phase. Mortars, MG nests, AT guns and obs posts go in the start area or round a finished FOB, and also take a phase to build. Spy planes, artillery, air strikes and paradrops are ready from phase 2.

MOVING

Each unit has its own speed: riflemen cover about 125 m a phase, tanks about twice that, more on roads. The order line is solid as far as the unit gets this phase, then grey to where it arrives later. Units carry on with an order in later phases. BEGIN warns you about units with no orders.

FIRE AND MOVEMENT

Fire pins men down: MGs and blasts most, cover less. Pinned men stop and shoot badly; badly pinned men stop shooting. Pin an enemy with MGs or mortars, then move round them. Smoke (from phase 1) blocks sight for about 30 s.

Orders: Shift-click sets a waypoint first, for a flank. SUPPRESS makes a unit stay put and fire on a spot, to pin whoever is there. FOLLOW sends it along behind another unit. DEFEND near a trench or sandbags mans them. A unit caught by surprise goes to ground and is slow to fire back. CAREFUL is slow, hard to pin and hard to surprise. RUSH is fast and keeps moving under fire but shoots worse. AMBUSH hides and holds fire until the enemy is close. FALL BACK pulls a unit back when it loses half its men. In battle, each tag shows what the unit is doing and its strength.

WINNING

Hold the enemy HQ ground for 40 seconds; a bar at the top shows the count. If nobody has won after 10 phases, the side holding more ground wins. An HQ under attack at the end gives up to three phases of overtime.

CAMPAIGN AND FRIENDS

The campaign is seven battles; each win unlocks more units. With a friend: HOST A GAME, send the code, and choose AGAINST ME or ON MY TEAM. Speed and pause change only when you both agree.

FOG OF WAR

You see only what your troops can see: line of sight, blocked by hills, buildings and woods. Troops in woods are seen only at half range. Snipers and obs posts see far. A spy plane sees everything near its path. Faded dots are where you last saw the enemy. The AI plays by the same rules.

TERRAIN

TOWNGarrison buildings: 85% cover, fire from roofs. Tank traps across streets.
OPENTrenches, wire and MG nests. Tanks cross it fast.
WOODSHidden, 35% cover. Tanks cannot enter.
WATEROnly bridges cross it. Demolish a bridge to cut a route.
ROADSTanks are faster. AT guns and tank traps stop them.
HILLSBlock sight. Firing downhill is more accurate. Obs posts see further.

CONTROLS

Click or drag a box to select your units. Pick ATTACK or DEFEND, then click the ground. Middle-drag pans, right-drag turns, scroll zooms. WASD pans, Q and E turn, Home resets the view. Right-click or Esc drops the tool. Space pauses. Delete removes what you placed this phase.
